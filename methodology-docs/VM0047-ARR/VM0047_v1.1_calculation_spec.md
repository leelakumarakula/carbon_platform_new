## 1. Scope

**Included:** the census-based approach (§1.2, §4.3), meaning direct planting at no more than 50 planting units per hectare on land that keeps its pre-project use. The calculation covers:
- woody biomass carbon above and below ground;
- burning and N-fertiliser emissions;
- uncertainty;
- removals;
- the AFOLU buffer.

**Not included:**
- The **area-based approach**. Its dynamic performance benchmark (§8.2, Appendix 1) needs annual remote-sensing stocking-index series for at least 30 project plots and the matched control plots, a 10-year history and donor-pool GIS layers. All of this comes from outside remote-sensing work, and the benchmark discounts every credit.
- **VMD0054 leakage.** It is not used, because census-based leakage is zero (§8.4).

## 2. Chain

1. **Census, per stratum.**
   - N = planting units in the complete census at t = 0 (§4.3(4)).
   - Density check: N ≤ 50 × stratum area.
2. **Sample, per stratum and campaign.** Each sampled planting unit is a sampling point, and the platform records:
   - alive (1) or dead / not relocated (0);
   - aboveground woody biomass (kg d.m.) from the documented allometric equation;
   - burned (1/0).

   M_t = dead / sampled.
3. **Carbon per unit (Eq. 25)** = B × (1 + R) × CF, with CF = 0.47. It is averaged over the **live** sampled units.
4. **Stock (Eqs. 24/22)** = N × (1 − M_t) × mean carbon per unit × 44/12, summed over strata. This is computed for the current campaign (t) and for the previous approved campaign (t − x). If the previous campaign has no biomass sample (the census at t = 0), its stock is 0.
5. **Uncertainty (Eqs. 29★–31)**, per stratum.
   - Relative half-width = √((T·SE/mean)² + U_M²).
   - U_M is from Eq. 31: T·√(M(1 − M)/(n − 1)) / (1 − M), as a fraction.
   - T = two-tailed 90 % Student-t with df = n − 1.
   - Half-widths of the strata are added in quadrature.
   - UNC = min(1, max(0, relative half-width − 0.10)).
   - If the half-width exceeds 100 % of the stock, no removals are credited (§8.5.2).
6. **Emissions (Eq. 12).**
   - Burning (Eqs. 26/27): B_burn = N × n_burn/n × previous mean biomass, then × COMF 1.0 × (GWP_CH4·EF_CH4 + GWP_N2O·EF_N2O) / 1000.
   - Fertiliser (Eqs. 15–21): direct (0.01), volatilisation (0.11 / 0.21, × 0.01) and leaching (0.24 × 0.011), each × 44/28 × 265.
7. **Removals (Eq. 33)**: CR = C_t·(1 − UNC_t) − C_{t−x}·(1 − UNC_{t−x}) − PE. Annualised removals: CR / years in the interval (Eq. 34★).
8. **Buffer** = max(rating, 12) % × the stock change after uncertainty (stock change only; never negative).
   - **VCUs** = floor(CR − buffer).
   - A negative result is reported as **REVERSAL**.

## 4. Data entry in the platform

- **Methodology version VM0047 / 1.1** → Calculation tab → module **VM0047 v1.1 - census-based approach**.
- **Per stratum** (Monitoring data → level *Stratum*): `V47_N` (units).
- **Sampling design:** one sampling point per sampled planting unit, using a stratified systematic sample of the census list. Per point (Monitoring data → level *Sampling point*):
  - `V47_ALIVE` (flag 1/0);
  - `V47_AGB` (kg d.m.; enter 0 for a dead unit, it is ignored);
  - `V47_BURNED` (flag 1/0).
- **Per farm:** `V47_FSN` and `V47_FON` (kg N, i.e. mass × N content).
- **Per project:** `V47_ROOT_SHOOT` (ratio, from IPCC 2019 Table 4.4 or ecoregional literature) and `V47_NPR` (%).
- **Two periods:** the census at t = 0 (approved) and the monitoring event, at most 5 years apart.
